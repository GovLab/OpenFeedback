BOSTON PERMIT SYSTEM - QUICK REFERENCE
Source: https://www.boston.gov/boston-permits-and-licenses

=== CONTACT INFO ===
Main Office: 1010 Massachusetts Avenue, Boston
Apply Online: Available for most permits at boston.gov

Fire Prevention: 617-343-3628 | bfdfirepreventioninfo@boston.gov
Inspectional Services (ISD): 617-635-5300 | isd@boston.gov
BOS:311 for general questions

=== ONLINE PORTALS ===
- Fire Prevention Portal (fire/safety permits)
- Inspectional Services Portal (building/electrical/plumbing)
- Can apply, pay, and print permits online
- Check permit status online
- No need to visit 1010 Mass Ave in person

=== COMMON PERMIT CATEGORIES ===
1. Home Renovations: Building, electrical, plumbing permits
2. Events & Entertainment: Place of assembly, temporary events, liquor licenses
3. Opening a Business: Use of premises, occupancy permits
4. Restaurant/Bar/Club: Multiple permits needed (liquor, food service, entertainment)
5. Construction/Contractors: Various trade permits, hot work, dumpsters
