HOME RENOVATION PERMITS - BOSTON
For homeowners renovating, remodeling, or making changes to their property

=== DO I NEED A PERMIT? ===

YES - You Need a Permit For:
- Kitchen remodels (if moving plumbing, electrical, or walls)
- Bathroom renovations (plumbing, electrical work)
- Finishing a basement
- Adding or removing walls
- Any work over $1,000
- New electrical circuits or panel upgrades
- Plumbing changes or additions
- Structural changes
- Adding a deck or porch
- Window/door replacement in historic districts

NO - Permit Usually Not Needed For:
- Painting walls
- Installing cabinets (non-structural)
- Replacing light fixtures (like-for-like)
- Flooring replacement
- Minor repairs under $1,000

When in doubt: Call ISD at 617-635-5300

=== TYPICAL HOME RENOVATION PERMITS ===

1. BUILDING PERMIT (Short Form)
   - For: Most interior renovations, non-structural work
   - Apply: ISD Portal online
   - Timeline: Usually approved within 1-2 weeks
   - Cost: Based on project value
   - Inspections: Typically 1-2 inspections needed
   - Common issues: Not sure if you need short or long form, missing drawings

2. BUILDING PERMIT (Long Form)
   - For: Major renovations, structural changes, additions
   - Examples: Removing load-bearing walls, adding a room, significant changes
   - May need: Architect/engineer stamped plans
   - Timeline: 2-6 weeks for review, complex projects longer
   - Common issues: Need professional drawings, zoning questions, longer wait times

3. ELECTRICAL PERMIT
   - For: New circuits, panel upgrades, outlet additions
   - Must use: Licensed electrician (DIY not allowed)
   - Apply: Electrician applies through ISD portal
   - Inspections: Rough (before closing walls) and Final
   - Timeline: Work can start immediately after approval
   - Common issues: Homeowners trying to DIY, coordinating inspection timing

4. PLUMBING PERMIT  
   - For: Moving fixtures, new pipes, water heater replacement
   - Must use: Licensed plumber
   - Apply: Plumber applies through ISD portal
   - Inspections: Rough and Final
   - Common issues: Gas work requires separate gas permit

5. GAS PERMIT
   - For: Gas line work, new gas appliances, gas water heaters
   - Must use: Licensed gas fitter
   - Apply: Through ISD portal
   - Safety critical: Very strict inspections

=== TYPICAL KITCHEN REMODEL ===
What You'll Need:
- Building permit (if removing/adding walls or changing layout)
- Electrical permit (new outlets, appliance circuits)
- Plumbing permit (if moving sink, adding dishwasher line)
- Gas permit (if gas stove/range)

Timeline: 2-4 weeks for permits, then construction can begin

=== TYPICAL BATHROOM REMODEL ===
What You'll Need:
- Building permit (for structural changes)
- Electrical permit (new lighting, outlets, vent fan)
- Plumbing permit (always needed - fixtures, pipes, drains)

Timeline: 2-3 weeks for permits

=== TYPICAL BASEMENT FINISHING ===
What You'll Need:
- Building permit (always required)
- Electrical permit (lighting, outlets, panel work)
- Plumbing permit (if adding bathroom)
- May need egress windows (fire safety)

Important: Basement bedrooms have special requirements

=== THE PROCESS ===
1. Decide on your project scope
2. Hire licensed contractors (electrician, plumber if needed)
3. Contractor prepares plans/drawings
4. Apply online through ISD portal (contractor usually does this)
5. Pay fees online
6. Wait for approval (1-6 weeks depending on complexity)
7. Start work ONLY after permit approved
8. Schedule inspections at key points
9. Pass final inspection before using space

=== INSPECTIONS ===
When to Call for Inspection:
- After rough work (before closing walls) - CRITICAL
- After final completion

How: Call ISD at 617-635-5300, give 24-48 hours notice

What happens if you fail: Fix the issues, call for re-inspection (may be extra fee)

=== COMMON PROBLEMS ===
1. "I already started work" - Can't permit after work is done, may face penalties
2. "Can I do my own electrical?" - No, must use licensed electrician in Boston
3. "The fees are confusing" - Based on project cost, ask ISD before applying
4. "How long will approval take?" - Simple: 1-2 weeks, Complex: 4-6+ weeks
5. "The contractor says we don't need a permit" - Verify with ISD, don't trust this
6. "I'm in a historic district" - Additional Landmarks Commission approval may be needed
7. "Inspector failed me" - Common issues: improper wiring, bad plumbing connections, need corrections

=== MONEY SAVING TIP ===
Get all permits together if doing multiple types of work. Coordinate with contractors 
to schedule inspections efficiently - don't close walls until rough inspections pass!

=== RED FLAGS (Don't Do This) ===
- Starting work before getting permits
- Hiring unlicensed contractors
- Trying to DIY electrical or plumbing to "save money"
- Ignoring inspection requirements
- Covering up work before inspection
