SPORTS FIELDS, COURTS & POOLS

=== COMMON ISSUES ===
- Field conditions: holes, bare patches, poor drainage, standing water after rain
- Court surfaces: cracks, faded lines, missing or broken nets and hoops
- Field and court lighting: lights off, broken, or shut off too early
- Scheduling: permitted leagues booking fields so drop-in play is impossible, unclear how to reserve
- Restrooms locked during games, no water fountains
- Demand for new uses: pickleball, futsal, cricket, skate spots
- Pools and beaches: short seasons, limited hours, lifeguard coverage, cleanliness

=== GOOD FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS ===
- Which field or court, and what sport?
- When does the problem happen (time of day, season, during permitted games)?
- Is it about the condition of the space, or about access and scheduling?
