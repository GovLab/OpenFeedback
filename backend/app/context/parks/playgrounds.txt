PLAYGROUNDS & SPLASH PADS

=== COMMON ISSUES ===
- Broken or missing equipment: swings, slides, climbing structures, spring riders
- Safety surfacing: worn rubber matting, compacted wood chips, exposed concrete under equipment
- Splash pads / spray decks turned off, clogged drains, short seasons
- No shade over play areas, equipment too hot in summer
- Not enough equipment for an age group (toddlers vs. older kids)
- Accessibility: no ramps or transfer platforms, inaccessible surfacing, no inclusive swings
- Fencing and gates (kids running toward streets, gates that don't latch)
- Missing benches for caregivers, no water fountains or restrooms nearby

=== GOOD FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS ===
- Which piece of equipment, and what exactly is wrong with it?
- What age are the kids who use it?
- Is it unsafe right now, or more of an improvement idea?
- Has it been taped off or reported?
