MAINTENANCE & SAFETY

=== COMMON ISSUES ===
- Overflowing trash cans, litter, broken glass, needles
- Graffiti and vandalism
- Overgrown grass and weeds, dead or damaged trees, fallen branches
- Broken benches, picnic tables, fences and gates
- Lighting: dark paths and entrances, broken fixtures
- Personal safety concerns, especially at night
- Dog waste, off-leash dogs outside dog areas
- Paths: cracked pavement, flooding, ice in winter, tripping hazards

=== GOOD FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS ===
- Where exactly in the park (entrance, path, playground, near which street)?
- How often does it happen, and for how long has it been going on?
- Was it reported to 311, and did anything happen afterward?
- Does it stop people from using the park? Who in particular?
