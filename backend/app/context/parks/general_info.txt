BOSTON PARKS & PLAYGROUNDS - QUICK REFERENCE
(Background for asking good follow-up questions. Do not quote phone numbers, hours or dates to residents.)

=== WHO RUNS WHAT ===
- Boston Parks and Recreation Department (BPRD): most city parks, playgrounds, athletic fields, courts and urban wilds; park maintenance, tree care, park event permits.
- Boston Centers for Youth & Families (BCYF): community centers, many indoor and outdoor pools, youth programs.
- Public Works: sidewalks, streets and street lighting around parks.
- Massachusetts DCR (state, not city): some large parks, beaches, parkways and the Charles River Esplanade. Feedback about these may need routing to the state.

=== HOW RESIDENTS REPORT ISSUES ===
- BOS:311 (call 311, the app, or the website) for broken equipment, litter, graffiti, lighting, fallen trees and other maintenance requests.
- 911 for emergencies or anything immediately dangerous.

=== USEFUL DETAILS TO ASK FOR ===
- Which park (name, nearest street or landmark) and which neighborhood
- Which feature (playground, field, court, path, restroom, lighting, trees...)
- How long the problem has existed, and whether it was reported to 311
- Who is affected (young kids, teens, seniors, people with disabilities, dog owners, teams)
- What would make it better
