PROGRAMS, EVENTS & COMMUNITY USE

=== COMMON TOPICS ===
- Ideas for programs: fitness classes, movie nights, concerts, youth sports, senior walking groups
- Park event permits: how to get one for a birthday party, cookout, festival or rally; cost and lead time
- Community gardens: waitlists, water access, plot conditions
- Friends-of-park groups and volunteering: cleanups, tree planting
- Communication: not knowing about construction, closures or renovation plans; wanting a say in redesigns
- Language access: signage and notices only in English

=== GOOD FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS ===
- What kind of program or event would they like, and for whom?
- Did they try to reserve or get a permit? What was hard about it?
- How would they like the city to communicate with them (signs, text, email, community meetings)?
