BOSTON HOUSING CONDITIONS & EXTREME HEAT - QUICK REFERENCE
(Background for asking good follow-up questions. Do not quote phone numbers, hours or dates to residents.)

=== WHO HANDLES WHAT ===
- Inspectional Services Department (ISD), Housing Division: inspections of rental housing for unsafe or unhealthy conditions, including mold, leaks, pests, heat and broken repairs; can order landlords to make repairs.
- Mayor's Office of Housing, including the Office of Housing Stability: rent increases, eviction notices, tenant rights and housing assistance.
- Office of Emergency Management: heat emergencies and cooling centers.
- Boston Public Health Commission: heat health guidance and outreach.
- Age Strong Commission: older residents, wellness checks, rides.
- Office of Neighborhood Services: neighborhood liaisons and community meetings.

=== HOW RESIDENTS REPORT ISSUES ===
- BOS:311 (call 311, the app, or the website) for housing complaints and inspection requests.
- 911 for emergencies or anyone in danger from heat.

=== USEFUL DETAILS TO ASK FOR ===
- Which neighborhood, street or building
- What is wrong, and how long it has been happening
- Who is affected (young children, older adults, people with health conditions)
- Whether the landlord, 311 or anyone else has been contacted, and what happened
