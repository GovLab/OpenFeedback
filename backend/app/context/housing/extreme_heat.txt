EXTREME HEAT

=== COMMON ISSUES ===
- Cooling centers too far away, closing too early, or hard to find
- Heat alerts not reaching residents, or only in English
- Older residents or people with disabilities stuck at home in hot apartments
- Streets, bus stops and play areas with no shade or trees

=== GOOD FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS ===
- Which neighborhood are you in, and which cooling center is closest?
- What would make it easier to stay cool (hours, location, transportation, information)?
- Is someone you know at risk right now?
