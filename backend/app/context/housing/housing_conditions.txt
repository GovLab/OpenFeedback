HOUSING CONDITIONS

=== COMMON ISSUES ===
- No working cooling during heat waves; broken AC or window units not replaced
- No heat or hot water in winter
- Mold, roof or plumbing leaks, water damage
- Mice, roaches, bedbugs; trash rooms not cleaned
- Broken locks, railings, outlets, elevators
- Landlord or property manager not responding to repair requests

=== GOOD FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS ===
- Which neighborhood or building is it in?
- How long has the problem been going on?
- Has the landlord been told, and did anyone respond?
- Is anyone in the home especially at risk (a baby, an older adult, someone with asthma)?
