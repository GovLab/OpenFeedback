LANGUAGE ACCESS, OLDER RESIDENTS & MOBILITY

=== COMMON ISSUES ===
- Alerts, notices and forms only in English
- No interpreter available when calling or at meetings
- Older residents who cannot walk to services; elevators out of order

=== GOOD FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS ===
- Which language would help, and for what information?
- Which neighborhood or building?
- What would make it easier to get help?
