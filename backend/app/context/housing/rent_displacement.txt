RENT, EVICTIONS & HOUSING ASSISTANCE

=== COMMON ISSUES ===
- Large rent increases, often without repairs
- Notices to vacate or non-renewal, sometimes after a tenant complained
- Not knowing tenant rights or where to get help
- Needing help paying for a cooling unit or other essentials

=== GOOD FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS ===
- Which neighborhood is the home in?
- When did the notice or increase arrive, and what does it say?
- Did it follow a complaint or repair request?
